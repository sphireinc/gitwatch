# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

gitwatch is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for contributors and users regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate professionally and assume good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and technical work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Help keep project spaces safe, focused, and inclusive.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, impersonation, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, release and support channels, and public spaces where someone is representing gitwatch or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `juan.sanchez@juanleonardosanchez.com`. Include links or screenshots and the minimum personal information needed to investigate. Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, may request additional context, and may remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Maintainers who are the subject of a report must recuse themselves from handling it where another trusted project owner is available.
